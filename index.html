The Price Curve

The Price Curve is a YouTube channel about money: what things cost, why prices move, and how people pay for them, built from public data with every figure sourced.

Channel: youtube.com/@ThePriceCurve

The Price Curve uploader

The uploader is a private tool the channel owner runs on his own computer. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel's own videos, set their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and schedule their release. It is used only by the channel owner, only for this channel, and is not offered to anyone else.
