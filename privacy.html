Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy covers "The Price Curve uploader" (the "App"), a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel The Price Curve to manage that channel.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the App accesses

With the channel owner's consent through Google sign-in (OAuth 2.0), the App accesses the owner's YouTube channel to upload the channel's own videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publishing schedule, and read back the status of those videos. It does not access, collect or store data about any other user.

Storage and sharing

The App stores its Google access token only on the channel owner's own computer. It sends data only to Google through the YouTube Data API. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. Deleting the stored token on the owner's computer removes the App's copy.
